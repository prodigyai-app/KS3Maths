# Prodigy KS3 Maths

Buildless revision site for Years 7–9, following the department planning sequence. Related classroom teaching periods are consolidated into focused revision lessons. All explanations and practice papers are original adaptations; the supplied slide decks, assessments and staff allocation records are not redistributed.

Rebuild content with `python scripts/build.py`. Open through a local HTTP server. Shared account and assignment assets are maintained in the ALevelPhysics platform repository.

Each year has two 30-mark original practice papers. Use teacher guidance for calculator use. Numerical checks verify a final value; method marks and extended reasoning use a transparent indicative scheme, with provisional server-side AI feedback when configured and signed in. Self-marking remains available.

All 142 lessons now use short swipeable slides with up to three explanation points or worked steps per slide. The 74 concept types each have an appropriate labelled visual, including fraction bars, number lines, algebra grids, graphs and geometry diagrams. Buttons and keyboard arrows also navigate the slides. Saved slide positions, word-card ratings and assessed answers use separate Maths course keys.

There are 290 definition cards, four questions per lesson and 49 mixed unit reviews. The graph plotter, twelve exploration models, optional Maths keyboard, six papers and original answer-field IDs remain available. The catalogue now contains 199 activity pages and 1,020 assessed question parts. Rebuild it with `node ../ALevelPhysics/platform/scripts/build-catalogs.cjs KS3Maths` from a checkout alongside the other subject repositories after rebuilding the course data.

The 24 October 2026 language review uses everyday explanations before formal Maths vocabulary, while retaining the department sequence and Year 9 preparation for KS4. The paper questions and graph-plotter guidance use the same plain-language approach. All original numeric answers and paper totals are preserved.
