# Prodigy KS3 Maths

Buildless revision site for Years 7–9. The main topic route follows **White Rose Maths secondary Version 3**, checked on 24 October 2026 against the publisher’s [2026 curriculum documentation](https://assets.whiteroseeducation.com/Resources/new-secondary-maths/supporting-materials/guidance/WRE%20Maths%20v3%20-%20Curriculum%20documentation.pdf) and [yearly overviews](https://assets.whiteroseeducation.com/resource-pages/secondary/Secondary%20maths%20overviews.pdf).

The main route has 52 blocks: 16 in Year 7, 17 in Year 8 and 19 in Year 9. The last two Year 9 blocks, simultaneous equations and trigonometry, are labelled optional. Eight additional department revision blocks remain available through “Show extra department revision”. Earlier challenge lessons, including formal nth terms in Year 7, remain accessible and are labelled separately from the main progression.

This is an independent revision companion. Its explanations, questions, papers and SVG/HTML diagrams are original Prodigy material. It follows block sequence and topic coverage and uses shared mathematical representation types; it does not reproduce every White Rose small-step resource, proprietary illustrations, worksheets or branding, and is not endorsed by White Rose Education. Supplied school slides, assessments and staff records are not redistributed.

All 245 lessons use swipeable slides, with at most three explanation points per slide. Lessons open with a complete worked example: up to four lines of working, with short notes alongside and optional method help underneath. On phones, notes sit directly below their working. Each of the 144 concept types has a labelled visual. Representations include equal-part bars, double number lines, place-value counters, zero pairs, algebra tiles, fraction area models, frequency trees, Venn diagrams, nets, compass constructions and coordinate transformations. Captions connect each model to a calculation or explanation. Buttons and keyboard arrows also navigate the decks.

There are 497 definition cards, four assessed questions per lesson and 60 mixed unit checks. New coverage includes money skills, data types, grouped data, ratio problems with a known difference, constructions, congruence, transformations, sets, expected outcomes and simultaneous equations. The graph plotter, optional Maths keyboard and twelve interactive models remain available.

Each year has one new original 30-mark review sampling the Version 3 route and two retained 30-mark department papers. Papers have saved working, optional timers and printable questions and schemes. No official grades or predictions are attached. Numerical checks verify a final value; method and reasoning marks use indicative schemes, with provisional server-side AI feedback when configured and signed in. Self-marking remains available.

All 142 existing lesson routes and all 1,020 previously published assessment parts retain their original IDs, wording, choices, numeric answers, marks and schemes. New questions append after old unit-check parts so stored answers and existing assignments keep their meaning. Slide positions, word-card ratings and assessed answers use separate Maths course keys. A regression fixture in the shared platform tests checks the previous assessment contract.

Maintain original concept content in scripts/content.py and scripts/revision.py. Calculation-first worked examples for all 144 concepts are authored in scripts/worked_examples.py; keep assessment content unchanged when editing them. The Version 3 block map and additional content are in scripts/white_rose.py; new yearly reviews are in scripts/route_papers.py. The review_skills property preserves the old unit-check question order while skills controls the revised lesson order. The source_page property links each main block to pages 74–125 of the 2026 guidance. The coverage.json file records the mapping and paper samples.

Rebuild with python scripts/build.py, then run node ../ALevelPhysics/platform/scripts/build-catalogs.cjs KS3Maths from this checkout alongside the other subject repositories. The resulting catalogue has 316 activity pages and 1,749 assessed question parts. Shared account, assignment and marking assets are maintained in the ALevelPhysics platform repository.
